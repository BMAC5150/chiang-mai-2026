Chang Klan Road runs straight past reception. Kalare, Anusarn, and the surrounding stalls are a two-minute walk — the easiest first-night market to open with.

Walking Streets

Weekend only
Must-see attractions
Sunday Walking Street
Tha Phae
Sundays Ratchadamnoen & Prapoklao Rd · Old City
4:00 pm – 10:30 pm

The city's biggest and most famous market. Focus: handmade crafts, Northern Thai textiles, local art, and a massive variety of unique street food you won't find elsewhere. Live street performances and open-air massage stalls line the route.

Saturday Wua Lai
Silver district
Saturdays Wua Lai Rd · near Chiang Mai Gate
4:00 pm – 10:00 pm

In the city's traditional silverworking district — famous for hand-tooled silver jewelry and metalwork. Generally smaller and slightly more relaxed than Sunday's, with a "local night out" feel.

Daily Markets

Any night of the week
Reliable options
Night Bazaar District
Right outside
Daily Chang Klan Rd (at the hotel)
5:00 pm – 11:00 pm

A cluster of connected markets including Kalare Night Bazaar and Anusarn Market. Best for souvenirs, clothing (including the ubiquitous elephant pants), and diverse food courts. You can also catch Muay Thai fights or cabaret shows in this district.

Chang Phuak North Gate
Food-first
Daily Outside North Gate of Old City moat
5:00 pm – midnight

A legendary food-first market. Famous for the "Cowboy Hat Lady" — Khao Kha Moo (stewed pork leg over rice) — and Suki Chang Phuak, a highly popular Thai suki stall.

Chiang Mai Gate South
Tasting walk
Daily Southern edge of the Old City moat
4:00 pm – midnight

A local favorite for a street-food tasting walk. Look for Northern Thai specialties like Khao Soi and Sai Ua (herb sausage).

Kad Na Mor · CMU
Student market
Daily Huay Kaew Rd · in front of CMU
5:00 pm – 10:00 pm

A trendy market with a youthful vibe — affordable student fashion and accessories, plus a food court with both Thai and international options (Japanese and Korean snacks among them).

Themed & Relaxed

Calmer alternatives
A quieter night
Kad Manee Night Market
Lakeside

A daily, food-focused market built around a small lake with plenty of open-air seating. Calmer and more local than the busier tourist centers.

Ploen Ruedee Night Market
Food garden

An international "food garden" near the Night Bazaar. Variety of food stalls, craft beer, and often live music or cultural performances.

Practical Tips

Cash, weather & more
Small things that matter

For the night out

  • Cash is king. Most vendors — especially at food stalls — are cash only. Carry plenty of 20 and 100 THB bills.
  • September rain. Carry a compact rain jacket or umbrella. Daily markets run even in light rain; weekend walking streets may pause briefly during heavy downpours. The covered arcades of the Night Bazaar are your best bet on a wet night.
  • Bargaining. Expect to negotiate prices for non-food items — but always with a friendly smile. It's part of the local social culture.
  • Getting around. Grab is the most convenient way to hop between markets, though the Old City markets are easily explored on foot once you arrive.
