Lanna specialty

A rare Lanna specialty — a therapist uses a small wooden mallet and wedge to gently tap along the body's sen energy lines. Rhythmic acoustic vibrations travel deep into muscle to release stubborn tension. Try it here — rarely performed well outside Northern Thailand.

Etiquette & Tips

Small courtesies · big comfort
Booking · pressure · shoes · tips

Six practical notes

  • Book ahead for premium spots. Fah Lanna, Zira, Oasis, and the ii Spa book out 1–2 days ahead — especially evenings. Facebook or WhatsApp works fast.
  • Speak up on pressure. If it's too intense, say Bao bao (“gently”). Therapists adjust immediately; they prefer knowing.
  • Dress is provided. Thai and herbal-compress sessions get loose pajama-style clothes. Oil massages: private room, sarong or towel, disposable undergarments.
  • Shoes off at the door — strict cultural rule. Bring slip-ons or sandals for a day of spa-hopping.
  • Avoid the neon parlors. Bright lights, staff calling from doorways late at night — not therapeutic. The venues above are the safe list.
  • Tipping. Appreciated, not required. ฿50–100 / hr at neighborhood shops, ฿100–200 / hr at high-end spas — hand cash directly to your therapist.
