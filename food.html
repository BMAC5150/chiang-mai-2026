Local Gems

Trusted & personal
Where the city eats
Thaan Aoan
Khao Soi
Old City · near Wat Chedi Luang
A trusted local gem for over 18 years. Highly recommended for authentic Khao Soi, Pad Thai, and Tom Yum Hot Pot. All-day breakfast alongside traditional Thai dishes in a relaxed setting.
Sia Fish Noodle
Family history
Chiang Mai
A special personal connection — where Mercy and her sisters served noodles and drinks during their childhood. Specialty: homemade fish balls, made daily from 100% pure sea fish with no preservatives.
Tong Tem Toh
Northern Thai
Nimman Soi 13
One of the most popular restaurants in the city for classic Northern Thai dishes. Be prepared for queues during dinner — it's a major local favorite.

Street Food & Markets

The essential mode
The most atmospheric eating
Legendary landmark

Khao Kha Moo Chang Phueak

"The Cowboy Hat Lady" · Chang Phuak (North) Gate

Her signature dish: braised pork leg over rice. A landmark of Chiang Mai street food — and it often sells out if you arrive late.

Suki Chang Phuak
Queue ticket
North Gate
The most famous Thai suki stall in the city. Expect a queue — and often a queue ticket.
Chiang Mai Gate Night Market
Food-first
South Gate
A hub perfect for a street-food tasting walk. Look for Northern hits like Sai Ua (herb sausage) and various spicy salads.
Sunday Walking Street
Craft & food
Tha Phae Gate route
Primarily crafts, but the temple courtyards along the route become vibrant food zones — coconut pancakes (kanom krok) and grilled shiitake skewers among the finds.

Unique Settings

Where the setting is the meal
Slow afternoons
Huay Tung Tao Reservoir
Bamboo huts
A truly local afternoon: dine in private bamboo huts built over the water. Order casual Thai dishes and drinks while looking out at the mountains.

Coffee

A serious scene
One place to start
Mercy's childhood home

Cafe Arte

Near Wat Jet Yod — a charming European-style café with a nostalgic atmosphere, specialty desserts, and a highly-rated "Dirty Coffee" that's worth ordering by name.

Cash is expected at local spots

Many local restaurants and market vendors are cash only. Carry a mix of 20 and 100 THB bills — small change moves fastest at busy stalls.
