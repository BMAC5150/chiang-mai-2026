The Monk's Trail

To Wat Pha Lat
Doi Suthep slopes
Monk's Trail · Wat Pha Lat
Gentle
Gradual uphill Slopes of Doi Suthep Half-day

A highly recommended "hidden gem". The trail winds gradually uphill through dense greenery, following a path traditionally used by monks. Moss-covered stone structures and peaceful meditation spaces offer a serene escape from the city noise.

Pha Dok Siew

Doi Inthanon National Park
Waterfalls & Karen village
Pha Dok Siew Nature Trail
Guided
4 km Downhill ~90 min from CNX

A downhill hike through evergreen forests and past the Sirithan Waterfall. Must be done with a local Karen hill tribe guide, who leads through beautiful rice fields to Mae Klang Luang Village.

The village grows locally grown organic coffee you can sample at the end — a small, quiet perk of finishing here.

Guide required — book ahead. This one is the strongest candidate for a full-day private van & driver pairing.

September Prep

Rainy-season notes
Small preparations, big difference
Kew Mae Pan is closed

The popular Kew Mae Pan Nature Trail in Doi Inthanon is closed from June to October for the rainy season to allow forest recovery. Don't route around it — just skip it this trip.

What to bring

  • Skip the heavy boots. They become waterlogged and hot. Bring high-quality hiking sandals or waterproof runners with non-slippery soles.
  • High socks and long trousers. Leeches are common in the woods and rice paddies during the rains — this is the simplest defense.
  • A light rain jacket or poncho. Heavy downpours are common in the late afternoon — pack it every day, even sunny mornings.
  • Repellent & sunscreen. Bring plenty of both — you'll want the repellent for the trail, and the sun still bites hard between showers.
Consider a private tour. A van and driver lets you set the pace — as much time as you like at the waterfalls and viewpoints, without the constraints of a group. Particularly good pairing for Doi Inthanon and Sticky Waterfalls, which are long days from the hotel.
